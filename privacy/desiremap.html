Privacy Policy — Desire Map

Effective: September 25, 2026 · v1.0.0 · com.sovereigndev.desiremap
Provided by Sovereign Developers ("we", "us", "our").

Your data stays on your device

Desire Map collects no data about you. Everything you map stays on your device.

  • The desires you map
  • Board posts (saved on this device only)
  • Reminders and guides progress
  • Feedback notes (saved on this device only)

What we collect

Nothing. Desire Map creates no account and asks for no personal information, sends nothing to any server for your content, and uses no analytics of its own. Deleting the app deletes your data.

Ads

Your content is never sent to any third party. The app does use Google AdMob to serve ads: AdMob may collect standard advertising data — including the Android advertising ID, approximate location, and ad-interaction data — to serve and measure ads, per Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). No account data, contact lists, or app content are shared with Google.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at https://sovereigndevelopers.org/privacy/desiremap.html.

Contact

Sovereign Developers — via the in-app feedback button.

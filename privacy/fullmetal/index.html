Privacy Policy

Full Metal

Last updated: July 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Full Metal is developed by Sovereign Developers. Full Metal is a performance-grade developer toolset designed for precision, speed, and complete control over your development environment.

2. Data We Collect

Full Metal collects and processes the following data:

  • Account Information: Email address and display name when you create an account.
  • Configuration Data: Tool preferences, environment settings, and workflow configurations stored locally on your device.
  • Usage Data: Feature usage statistics to improve tooling performance.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and app version for compatibility.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Provide and maintain developer tooling functionality.
  • Optimize performance for your specific device and environment.
  • Improve features and user experience.
  • Provide technical support when requested.

4. Data Storage

Full Metal follows a local-first architecture. Your configurations and workflow data are stored locally on your device. Account credentials are stored securely for authentication.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We do not share your data with advertisers. Your development configurations remain private and under your control.

6. Third-Party Services

Full Metal does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. All processing is performed locally or on sovereign infrastructure owned by Sovereign Developers.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Export your configurations at any time.
  • Opt out of usage analytics.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security measures. Local data leverages device-level encryption when available.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at: Sovereign Developers via sovereigndevelopers.org.

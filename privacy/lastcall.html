Privacy Policy

Last Call

Effective date: September 25, 2026

1. Summary

Last Call (Android package com.sovereigndev.lastcall) is published by Sovereign Developers. Last Call collects no data about you. Everything you plan stays on your device.

2. What We Collect

Nothing. Last Call:

  • creates no account and asks for no personal information;
  • sends nothing to any server — there is no network access for your content;
  • uses no analytics of its own; the Google AdMob SDK serves ads and may use advertising identifiers as described under Third parties.

3. What Stays on Your Device

Your farewell events, guest lists, RSVPs, toasts, memory books, and feedback notes are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. Invites go out by you — the app never messages anyone on its own.

4. Third Parties

Your content (farewells, events, and everything you plan) is never sent to any third party.

The app does use Google AdMob to serve ads: AdMob may collect standard advertising data — including the Android advertising ID, approximate location, and ad-interaction data — to serve and measure ads, per Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

No account data, contact lists, or app content are shared with Google.

5. Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at https://sovereigndevelopers.org/privacy/lastcall.html.

7. Contact

Sovereign Developers — via the in-app feedback button.

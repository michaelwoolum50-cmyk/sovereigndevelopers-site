Privacy Policy

Echo

Last updated: July 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Echo is developed by Sovereign Developers. Echo is a sovereign content distribution engine designed to amplify your voice across multiple channels from a single, unified platform.

2. Data We Collect

Echo collects and processes the following data:

  • Account Information: Email address and display name when you create an account.
  • Content Data: Posts, messages, and media you create and distribute through the app, stored locally on your device.
  • Channel Connections: OAuth tokens for connected social accounts (used solely to post content on your behalf — we never read or store your private messages from connected platforms).
  • Usage Data: Distribution patterns and engagement metrics to optimize content delivery.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and app version for compatibility.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Distribute your content across connected channels.
  • Track engagement and reach of your distributed content.
  • Improve distribution algorithms and user experience.
  • Provide customer support when requested.

4. Data Storage

Echo follows a local-first architecture. Your content and distribution preferences are stored locally on your device. Account information and OAuth tokens are stored securely on our servers using industry-standard encryption.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Content is shared only to the channels you explicitly connect and authorize. We do not share your data with advertisers or analytics networks.

6. Third-Party Services

Echo connects to third-party social platforms via OAuth when you explicitly authorize them. We only request posting permissions and never access your private messages or personal data from connected platforms. Echo does not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Disconnect any third-party channel at any time.
  • Export your content at any time.
  • Opt out of usage analytics.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest. OAuth tokens are encrypted and stored securely. Local data leverages device-level encryption when available.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at: Sovereign Developers via sovereigndevelopers.org.

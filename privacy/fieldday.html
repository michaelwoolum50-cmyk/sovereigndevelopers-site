Privacy Policy

FieldDay

Effective date: September 27, 2026

1. Overview

FieldDay (Android package com.sovereigndev.fieldday) is published by Sovereign Developers ("we", "us", "our"). FieldDay is an outdoor sports and field companion built around twelve on-device modules: CreelBook (fishing log), HuntLog (hunt log), StandLog (stand log), TackleTutor (tackle guidance), Trail Prep AI and TrailSafe AI (trail planning and safety), ParkStamp (park visits), PicklePlay (pickleball), SwingAI (swing analysis), BeltPath (belt progression), LaneLedger (bowling lane log), and BackyardScorer (backyard game scoring). The app is fully on-device: it requires no account, no login, and no sign-up, and we operate no servers that receive your content. Everything you create or track in FieldDay stays on your device.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any server of ours. We run no analytics SDKs and no crash-reporting services of our own. The only third-party data collection in the app comes from the advertising library described below.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

FieldDay is free and supported by ads. It displays banner and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve, measure, and protect against fraud in these ads, the AdMob SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • Advertising ID — the resettable Android advertising identifier used for ad personalization and frequency capping;
  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, app version, and app interaction signals used for ad delivery and measurement;
  • Coarse location signals — approximate location derived from IP address, used only for ad serving and fraud prevention;
  • Diagnostics related to ad delivery — such as ad impressions, clicks, and viewability, used for billing and reporting.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. How Information Is Used

The advertising data described above is used solely to provide and measure the ads that keep FieldDay free. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share data with any other third parties.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we operate no accounts and receive none of your data, there is no server-side data for us to retain or delete. Your app content lives only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. To limit ad-related data held by Google, reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings.

6. Children's Privacy

FieldDay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads shown to users identified as under 13 are served by Google on a non-personalized basis. If you believe a child has provided us with information directly, please contact us and we will address it.

7. Security

Since your content never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security (lock screen, encryption). No method of transmission over the Internet is relevant here because we transmit nothing.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted at this page, and the effective date above will be revised.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about FieldDay, contact Sovereign Developers through the contact options listed at sovereigndevelopers.org or through the developer contact details on the app's Google Play Store listing.

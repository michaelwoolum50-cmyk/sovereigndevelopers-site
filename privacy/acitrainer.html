Privacy Policy — ACI Trainer

Last updated: September 24, 2026
Provided by Sovereign Developers ("we", "us", "our").

1. Overview

ACI Trainer is a training tool: pick one of 12 professional paths, study the cards, and run practice drills to train your AI to field scope. The app does not certify, credential, license, or accredit anything.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ACI Trainer collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using ACI Trainer, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • No account, no sign-in: ACI Trainer has no accounts. We do not collect your name, email address, or password;
  • On-device training progress: your AI profiles, study progress, and quiz answers are stored on your device only (local storage). This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it;
  • Device information: device model, operating system, and app version used for app functionality and diagnostics;
  • Purchase activity: plan and subscription purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not see or store your payment details; purchase records are handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Device Permissions

ACI Trainer does not request access to your photos, camera, location, contacts, or microphone. No device permissions are required to use the app.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve ACI Trainer and its features;
  • Unlock plan entitlements you purchase (verified through Google Play);
  • Keep the app secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your training content to train advertising models.

5. Advertising

Our free tier is supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob to serve ads. AdMob and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, coarse location, and app interaction data to show you personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure ad performance. This collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Paid plans (Starter, Professional, Business, Founding, and One-Shot) remove all ads.

You can opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization (Android). You can also reset your advertising ID there.

6. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use Google Firebase services (including Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) to understand how our apps are used and to diagnose crashes. These services collect aggregated, de-identified information such as device model, operating system version, app version, session length, and screens visited. Crash reports may include technical diagnostics needed to fix the problem.

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers: with companies that help us operate the app (advertising networks, analytics and crash-reporting providers, app stores, payment processors), bound by contract to use the data only for our purposes;
  • Legal compliance: when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the public;
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you.

8. Data Retention

Your training progress lives on your device only — we hold no copy. Uninstalling the app, clearing app data, or using Settings > Erase my progress removes it. Purchase records are kept by Google Play under Google's policies.

9. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Because your data never leaves your device, deletion is immediate and entirely in your control:

  • In the app: use Settings > Erase my progress to wipe all training data on the device, or uninstall the app;
  • By email: write to michaelwoolum50@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" for anything we may hold;
  • Deletion page: see our step-by-step guide at sovereigndevelopers.org/data-deletion.

We respond to deletion requests within 30 days.

10. Children’s Privacy

ACI Trainer is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify you inside the app or by email.

Privacy Policy — DeepWorkMode

Effective: September 25, 2026 · v1.1.0 · com.sovereigndev.deepworkmode
Provided by Sovereign Developers ("we", "us", "our").

Your data stays on your device

DeepWorkMode works entirely on your device. Everything you create in the app is stored in the app's private on-device storage and is never transmitted anywhere. No accounts exist. The app itself collects no data about you.

  • Focus sessions — task names, timers, completion status, and surfed urges
  • Scheduled blocks — times and task names
  • Settings — session length, ad toggle
  • Feedback — saved on this device only; we only see it if you copy it out and send it to us

Export / Restore

Export / Restore move your data only where you put it (a file download, your clipboard, a backup file you chose).

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions for its own features. Because the app is free and ad-supported, its manifest declares the standard Google Mobile Ads SDK permissions so ads can load: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and advertising-ID permissions (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID / ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID). These exist only to serve ads — the app has no location permission, no camera, no storage access, and cannot reach anything outside its own private data.

Ads

DeepWorkMode is free and supported by advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers for advertising (such as the Android advertising ID) and approximate location for ad targeting, to serve and measure ads. That traffic goes only to Google's ad servers. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Banners appear only in clearly labeled slots on the Focus, Plan, Feedback, and Manual pages — never inside a fullscreen focus session. Interstitials appear only at natural breaks (after a data export or a saved feedback note), capped at one per 3 minutes.

You control them: Plan tab → Ads → "Show ads" toggle turns ads off completely.

Children

DeepWorkMode is a general productivity app: no accounts, no chat, no public user-generated content.

Contact

Sovereign Developers. Questions about this policy: use the in-app feedback (saved on your device — copy it out to send it to us) or the support channel on the Play Store listing.

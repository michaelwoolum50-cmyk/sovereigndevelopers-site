Privacy Policy

MedCabinet

Effective date: September 25, 2026

1. Summary

MedCabinet (Android package com.sovereigndev.medcabinet) is published by Sovereign Developers. MedCabinet collects no data about you. Your medication information — the most sensitive data this app touches — never leaves your device.

2. What We Collect

Nothing. MedCabinet:

  • creates no account and asks for no personal information;
  • sends nothing to any server — the drug reference and interaction engine are bundled offline;
  • uses no analytics of its own; the Google AdMob SDK serves ads and may use advertising identifiers as described under Third parties.

3. What Stays on Your Device

Profiles, medication lists, expiry dates, pill counts, dose logs, the toss list, feedback notes, and the optional privacy PIN are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. Exports you create (caregiver summaries) are written to your device's Documents/MedCabinet folder only when you tap export.

4. Third Parties

Your content (cabinet inventory, dose logs, and summaries) is never sent to any third party.

The app does use Google AdMob to serve ads: AdMob may collect standard advertising data — including the Android advertising ID, approximate location, and ad-interaction data — to serve and measure ads, per Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

No account data, contact lists, or app content are shared with Google.

5. Health Data

Medication and health-related information you enter is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted, shared, sold, or used for advertising.

6. Medical Disclaimer

MedCabinet is informational only — not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe.

7. Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at https://sovereigndevelopers.org/privacy/medcabinet.html.

9. Contact

Sovereign Developers — via the in-app feedback button.

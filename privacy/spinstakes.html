Privacy Policy — SpinStakes

Effective: September 25, 2026 · v1.0.0 · com.sovereigndev.spinstakes
Provided by Sovereign Developers ("we", "us", "our").

Summary

SpinStakes is a free slots game with sweepstakes drawings. No accounts, no analytics, no data collection. The only network content is advertising (Google AdMob).

What stays on your device (never transmitted)

All of the following lives in the app’s private on-device storage and is never transmitted anywhere:

  • Coin wallet — coin balance, lifetime spins
  • Ticket ledger — entry tickets earned and which drawing each was assigned to
  • Settings — machine choice, age-gate confirmation
  • Drawings state — countdown display data cached locally

The app requests zero dangerous Android permissions. Because the app is free and ad-supported, its manifest declares the standard Google Mobile Ads SDK permissions so ads can load: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and advertising-ID permissions (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID / ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID). These exist only to serve ads — the app has no location permission, no camera, no storage access, and cannot reach anything outside its own private data.

Advertising

SpinStakes is free and supported by advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers for advertising (such as the Android advertising ID) and approximate location for ad targeting, to serve and measure ads. That traffic goes only to Google’s ad servers. See Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Banners appear only on the Drawings, Wallet, and Settings tabs — never on the slot machines while spinning. Interstitials appear only at natural breaks (every 25th spin, after buying a ticket), capped at one per 10 minutes.

Sweepstakes

Drawings are free to enter — entries are earned by playing only. No purchase is ever necessary and nothing is ever sold in the app. Winners are drawn on the advertised dates from all valid entries. You must be 18 or older to hold entries; void where prohibited. Full official rules for each drawing are in the app’s Rules tab.

No purchases

SpinStakes sells nothing. There are no in-app purchases, no coin sales, and no way to pay for entries — by design. Keeping money out is what keeps the drawings a legal sweepstakes.

Children

SpinStakes is for ages 18 and up only (sweepstakes eligibility). This app is not for children and is not enrolled in Designed for Families.

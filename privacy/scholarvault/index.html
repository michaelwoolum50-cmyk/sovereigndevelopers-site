Privacy Policy

ScholarVault Edge

Last updated: July 18, 2026

1. Introduction

ScholarVault Edge is developed by Sovereign Developers. We believe privacy is a fundamental right. ScholarVault Edge is designed as a local-first, sovereign AI education application — meaning your data stays on your device whenever possible.

2. Data We Collect

ScholarVault Edge collects and processes the following data:

  • Account Information: Email address and display name when you create an account.
  • Educational Content: Your study materials, notes, and learning progress stored locally on your device.
  • Usage Data: App usage patterns and feature engagement to improve the learning experience.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and app version for compatibility and support.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Provide and maintain the AI tutoring functionality.
  • Personalize your learning experience.
  • Improve app performance and features.
  • Provide customer support when requested.

4. Data Storage

ScholarVault Edge is built on a sovereign, local-first architecture. Educational content and learning progress are stored locally on your device. Account information is stored securely on our servers for authentication purposes only.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We do not share your data with advertisers. Your educational content remains private and under your control at all times.

6. Third-Party Services

ScholarVault Edge does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. All AI processing is done locally on your device or on sovereign infrastructure owned and controlled by Sovereign Developers.

7. Children's Privacy

ScholarVault Edge is an educational application. We comply with applicable child privacy protection laws (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect more information than necessary to provide the educational service. Parents may contact us to review or delete their child's data at any time.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Export your educational content at any time.
  • Opt out of usage analytics.

9. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. Local data is stored using device-level encryption when available.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: Sovereign Developers via our website at sovereigndevelopers.org.

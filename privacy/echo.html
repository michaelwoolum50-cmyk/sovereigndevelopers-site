Privacy Policy — Echo

Last updated: September 22, 2026
Provided by Sovereign Developers ("we", "us", "our").

1. Overview

Echo is a music and entertainment app for discovering tracks, building playlists, and sharing what you are listening to.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Echo collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Echo, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password when you create an account;
  • Your library: playlists, favorites, follows, and listening history you create in the app;
  • Device information: device model, operating system, app version, and audio-output settings;
  • Usage data: plays, skips, and searches, used to personalize recommendations.

3. Device Permissions

Echo may request access to notifications (new releases and playlist updates) and media/storage (offline downloads where offered). Permissions can be changed anytime in device settings.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve Echo and its features;
  • Create and manage your account and sign you in;
  • Respond to your requests and provide customer support;
  • Keep the app secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms;
  • Send service-related messages (account, security, and feature updates);
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your personal content to train advertising models, and we do not sell your personal information.

5. Advertising

Our free apps are supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob to serve ads. AdMob and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, coarse location, and app interaction data to show you personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure ad performance. This collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization (Android). You can also reset your advertising ID there.

6. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use Google Firebase services (including Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) to understand how our apps are used and to diagnose crashes. These services collect aggregated, de-identified information such as device model, operating system version, app version, session length, and screens visited. Crash reports may include technical diagnostics needed to fix the problem.

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers: with companies that help us operate the app (hosting, analytics, crash reporting, advertising), bound by contract to use the data only for our purposes;
  • Legal compliance: when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the public;
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you.

8. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide the app and for legitimate business purposes. Account information is kept while your account is active. Content you delete in the app is removed from our active systems within 30 days. We may retain limited records where required by law (for example, transaction or safety records).

9. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time:

  • In the app: use Settings > Privacy or Settings > Delete Account where available;
  • By email: write to michaelwoolum50@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and the email address tied to your account;
  • Deletion page: see our step-by-step guide at sovereigndevelopers.org/data-deletion.

We respond to deletion requests within 30 days. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile and content; some anonymized analytics may remain.

10. Children’s Privacy

Echo is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify you inside the app or by email.

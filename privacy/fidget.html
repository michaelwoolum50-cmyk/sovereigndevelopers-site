Privacy Policy — Fidget

Effective: September 25, 2026 · v1.1.0 · com.sovereigndev.fidget
Provided by Sovereign Developers ("we", "us", "our").

Your data stays on your device

Fidget works entirely on your device. Everything you create is stored in the app's private on-device storage and is never transmitted anywhere. No accounts, no sign-in. The app itself collects no data about you.

  • Toy collection — toys you add, edit, or delete
  • Challenges board — your posts and likes (the two sample posts shipped with the app are clearly labeled)
  • Reminders — titles, dates, notes
  • Sessions — bubble pops and finished breathing rounds (for your streaks)
  • Feedback — saved on this device only; we only see it if you copy it out and send it to us

Export / Import

Export / Import move your data only where you put it.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions for its own features. Because the app is free and ad-supported, its manifest declares the standard Google Mobile Ads SDK permissions so ads can load: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and advertising-ID permissions (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID / ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID). These exist only to serve ads — the app has no location permission, no camera, no storage access, and cannot reach anything outside its own private data.

Ads

Fidget is free and supported by advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers for advertising (such as the Android advertising ID) and approximate location for ad targeting, to serve and measure ads. That traffic goes only to Google's ad servers. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Banners appear only on the Home, Settings, and Manual tabs — never on the bubble-popping Play tab, the Calm breathing tab, the Toys collection, the Challenges board, or the feedback sheet. Interstitials appear only at a natural break (after saving a new toy), capped at one per 10 minutes.

Health note

Fidget's bubble games and breathing exercises may help you feel calmer — they are not treatment. Nothing in this app is medical advice. If anxiety ever feels overwhelming, reach out to someone you trust or a local crisis line.

Children

Fidget is recommended for ages 13 and up. It is not enrolled in Designed for Families: no accounts, no chat, no public user-generated content (board posts stay on the device; sample posts are labeled).

Contact

Sovereign Developers. Questions about this policy: use the in-app feedback (saved on your device — copy it out to send it to us) or the support channel on the Play Store listing.

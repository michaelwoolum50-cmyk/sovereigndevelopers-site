Privacy Policy

Forever Us

Last updated: July 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Forever Us is developed by Sovereign Developers. Forever Us is a digital legacy platform designed to capture, protect, and pass down your memories, stories, and family history for generations.

2. Data We Collect

Forever Us collects and processes the following data:

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, and password when you create an account.
  • Legacy Content: Photos, videos, written memories, audio recordings, and family history documents you upload and create within the app.
  • Family Connections: Names and relationships of family members you choose to link to your legacy content.
  • Usage Data: Feature usage patterns to improve the legacy preservation experience.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and app version for compatibility and support.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Store, organize, and present your legacy content and family memories.
  • Enable sharing of legacy content with family members you designate.
  • Preserve your content for future generations as you direct.
  • Improve features and user experience.
  • Provide customer support when requested.

4. Data Storage

Forever Us follows a local-first, sovereign architecture. Your legacy content is stored locally on your device and synchronized securely to sovereign infrastructure owned by Sovereign Developers. Your memories are never stored on third-party cloud services.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We do not share your data with advertisers. Legacy content is shared only with family members you explicitly designate within the app. You control who sees what — always.

6. Third-Party Services

Forever Us does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. All data processing and storage is performed on sovereign infrastructure owned and controlled by Sovereign Developers.

7. Children's Privacy

Forever Us is designed for users of all ages to preserve family memories. When family content includes information about children, that information is controlled by the account holder (parent or guardian). We comply with applicable child privacy protection laws (COPPA). Parents may contact us to review or delete their child's information at any time.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data and legacy content.
  • Delete your account and all associated data permanently.
  • Export your legacy content at any time.
  • Control who can view and access your shared content.
  • Opt out of usage analytics.

9. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest. Your legacy content is encrypted on-device and during synchronization. Only you and the family members you authorize can access your content.

10. Data Retention

Your legacy content is retained for as long as your account is active. If you choose to delete your account, all associated content will be permanently deleted within 30 days, unless you have designated a family successor to inherit the account.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at: Sovereign Developers via sovereigndevelopers.org.

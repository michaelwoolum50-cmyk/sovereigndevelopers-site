Data Deletion

Last updated: July 28, 2026

To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data from any Sovereign Developers application, send an email to the address below.

Email us at:

support@sovereigndevelopers.org

Include in Your Email

  • The email address associated with your account.
  • The name of the app you want your data deleted from (or say "all apps" for everything).
  • State that you want your account and data deleted.

What Happens Next

  • You will receive a confirmation email within 24 hours.
  • Your account and all data are permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Deleted data cannot be recovered.

This action is irreversible. All data is permanently removed within 30 days of request approval.

What Gets Deleted

  • Account credentials — email, password, display name
  • User-generated content — all posts, journals, entries, uploads, and media
  • App data — preferences, settings, usage history
  • Connected accounts — OAuth tokens for any linked third-party services
  • Subscription data — any active subscription is cancelled at end of billing cycle

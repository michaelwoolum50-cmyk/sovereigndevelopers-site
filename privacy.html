Last updated: September 22, 2026

Sovereign Developers ("we", "us", "our") builds and publishes mobile apps. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information across our apps and this website. Each app also has its own detailed privacy policy:

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the app, we collect:

  • Account information you provide (name, email address, password) when you register;
  • Content you create in the app (profiles, posts, messages, journals, photos, collections);
  • Device and usage information collected automatically (device model, OS version, app version, feature usage, crash diagnostics);
  • Advertising identifiers via Google AdMob in our free, ad-supported apps;
  • Permissions you grant such as photos, camera, location, or notifications — always optional and revocable in device settings.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to operate and improve our apps, manage accounts, provide support, keep our services secure, comply with the law, and — in free apps — to show ads via Google AdMob. We do not sell personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted providers to run our apps, including Google AdMob (advertising), Google Firebase (analytics and crash reporting), and Google Play services. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies, including Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed to provide our services or as required by law. Deleted accounts and content are removed from active systems within 30 days.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time. See our Data Deletion page for step-by-step instructions, or email michaelwoolum50@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We respond within 30 days.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, except where a parent or guardian creates and manages the account (for example, Chore Root). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent, and we delete such information promptly if discovered.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy and will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be announced in-app or by email.

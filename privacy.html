Each product may also have its own detailed privacy page, which adds product-specific detail on top of this policy. Where a product page and this policy differ, the product page applies to that product:

1. Information We Collect

What we collect depends on the product you use and the choices you make. The categories below apply across our whole portfolio.

Things you provide to us

  • Account information — name, email address, and password when you register; a phone number if you choose to add one;
  • Payment information — where you buy something from us, processed by our payment providers;
  • Support messages — what you tell us when you contact us for help.

Content you create

Depending on the product: profiles, posts, messages, journals, photos, videos, files, collections, records, and anything else you save or share in our products.

Device and technical information

  • Device model, operating system and version, app or browser version, language settings;
  • IP address, crash reports, diagnostics, and records of how features are used;
  • We collect this when our products contact our servers — for example, when you install an update, sync your data, or open the app.

Activity

Which features you use, searches you run, content you view or interact with, and purchase activity — used to operate the product and understand how it is used.

Location

Only in products with location features, and only when you grant permission. You can revoke it any time in your device settings.

Advertising identifiers

In our free, ad-supported products, advertising networks collect device advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. You can reset or limit these in your device settings.

Information from other sources

We may receive information from publicly accessible sources and from partners — for example, app stores confirming a purchase, or analytics providers summarizing usage. We use technologies such as cookies, local storage, pixel tags, and server logs to collect and store information.

2. Why We Collect Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes, across all products and platforms:

  • Provide our services — run the product, manage your account, sync your data, process purchases;
  • Maintain and improve — keep things working, fix crashes, troubleshoot problems you report, and make features better;
  • Develop new products — what we learn from existing products helps us design new ones;
  • Personalize content and ads — tailor what you see, and — in free products — show ads. We do not build ad profiles from sensitive categories such as race, religion, sexual orientation, or health;
  • Measure performance — analytics that tell us how our products and ads perform;
  • Communicate with you — security alerts, product changes, and answers when you contact us;
  • Protect everyone — detect and stop fraud, abuse, spam, malware, and security risks.

We ask for your consent before using your information for any purpose not described here.

3. Your Privacy Controls

  • In-product settings — review and update your account, profile, and preferences;
  • Device permissions — photos, camera, location, notifications, and similar access are always optional and revocable in your device settings;
  • Ad choices — reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalization in your device settings;
  • Access, correction, deletion — request a copy of your data, fix it, or delete it at any time (see Data Deletion).

4. When We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these cases:

  • With your consent;
  • With service providers — companies that help us run our products (advertising networks, analytics and crash-reporting providers, cloud hosting, payment processors, app stores). They may only use it to provide their service to us and are governed by their own privacy policies;
  • For legal reasons — to comply with the law or a valid legal request, or to protect rights, safety, and property;
  • Aggregated or anonymized — statistics that cannot identify you may be shared publicly or with partners.

5. Third-Party Services

Across our portfolio we use trusted providers including advertising networks (such as Google AdMob), analytics and crash-reporting providers (such as Google Firebase), app distribution platforms (such as Google Play and the Apple App Store), payment processors, and cloud hosting providers. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies, including Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Deleted accounts and content are removed from our active systems within 30 days; backups roll off within 90 days. See Data Deletion for details.

7. Security

We protect information with safeguards including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and limited internal access. No system is perfectly secure, but we work continuously to protect your information.

8. International Data Transfers

Our servers and providers may be located outside the country where you live, and your information may be processed there. Data protection laws vary by country; regardless of where your information is processed, we apply the protections described in this policy.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our products are not directed to children under 13, except where a parent or guardian creates and manages the account (for example, Chore Root). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent, and we delete such information promptly if we discover it.

10. U.S. State Privacy Rights

Some U.S. state laws require specific disclosures and grant residents specific rights:

  • The categories of information we collect and their sources are described in Section 1;
  • The purposes for collection and use are described in Section 2;
  • Sharing is described in Section 4 — we do not sell personal information;
  • Retention is described in Section 6;
  • You may request to know, correct, or delete your personal information at any time via our Data Deletion page or the email below. We respond within 30 days and will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy and will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be announced in our products or by email.

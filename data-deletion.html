Data Deletion

Last updated: September 22, 2026

You can delete your account and personal data from any Sovereign Developers app at any time. Choose whichever method is easiest:

Option 1 — In the App (fastest)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (or your Profile).
  3. Tap Privacy or Delete Account and confirm.

Your profile, content, and account data are queued for permanent deletion immediately.

Option 2 — Email Request

  1. Email michaelwoolum50@gmail.com from the address tied to your account.
  2. Use the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  3. Tell us the app name (for example, Forever Us) and the account email or username.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account profile and sign-in credentials;
  • Content you created (posts, messages, journals, photos, collections, chore records);
  • Preferences and app settings tied to your account.

What May Be Retained

  • Anonymized analytics that cannot identify you;
  • Records we must keep by law (for example, transaction or safety records), kept only as long as legally required;
  • Backups, which roll off within 90 days.

Timing

We complete deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email once done. In-app deletions begin immediately and finish within 30 days.

Questions

Contact michaelwoolum50@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Question".

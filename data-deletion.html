This page automatically covers every future product we publish — if it is ours, this page covers it, on every platform where it appears.

One request covers every platform. If you used the same account in our Android app and on the web, a single deletion request deletes your data from both.

Option 1 — In the Product (fastest)

  1. Open the app or website and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (or your Profile).
  3. Choose Privacy or Delete Account and confirm.

Your profile, content, and account data are queued for permanent deletion immediately. (The exact wording of the menu varies slightly by product.)

Option 2 — Email Request

  1. Email michaelwoolum50@gmail.com from the address tied to your account.
  2. Use the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  3. Tell us the product name (for example, Scholar Vault) and the account email or username.

No Account?

If you never created an account, there is no account data tied to you to delete. Advertising identifiers stored on your own device can be reset or limited in your device settings at any time.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account profile and sign-in credentials;
  • Content you created (posts, messages, journals, photos, videos, files, collections, records);
  • Preferences and settings tied to your account;
  • Across every platform where you used the account.

What May Be Retained

  • Anonymized analytics that cannot identify you;
  • Records we must keep by law (for example, transaction or safety records), kept only as long as legally required;
  • Backups, which roll off within 90 days.

Timing

We complete deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email once done. In-product deletions begin immediately and finish within 30 days.

Questions

Contact michaelwoolum50@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Question". See also our Privacy Policy.

About SpinStakes

SpinStakes is a free slot-machine app with five machines to play. Spins earn coins, coins turn into sweepstakes tickets, and you pick which drawing your tickets go into. Monthly drawings for real prizes, funded by ads — and every winner is posted publicly in the app. No purchases, no paid coins, ever.

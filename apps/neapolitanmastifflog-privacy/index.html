Privacy Policy

NeapolitanMastiffLog

Effective date: September 29, 2026

1. Data Sovereignty & Storage

NeapolitanMastiffLog (Android package com.michael.neapolitanmastifflog) is operated by Michael's Sovereign Apps ("we", "us", or "our"). NeapolitanMastiffLog operates with a strict offline-first, local-storage architecture. All Neapolitan Mastiff lineage records, facial fold hygiene logs, estate guarding shifts, and canine profiles are stored exclusively in your device's local application storage. We do not transmit, collect, or store your private pastoral records on remote servers.

2. Advertising & Monetization

NeapolitanMastiffLog integrates Google AdMob (Publisher Profile pub-7214805939162450) to display non-intrusive mobile advertisements. AdMob may process non-personally identifiable diagnostic device telemetry and advertising identifiers in compliance with Google Play and international privacy frameworks. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Permissions

The application requests minimal permissions strictly necessary for rendering on-device interfaces, capturing dog photos, and loading ad inventory:

  • android.permission.CAMERA (for private dog photos)
  • android.permission.INTERNET (for AdMob ad delivery)
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (for connectivity verification)
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (for advertising ID compliance)

4. Children's Privacy

NeapolitanMastiffLog does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

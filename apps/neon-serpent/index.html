About Neon Serpent: Snake Arena

Neon Serpent drops you into a glowing snake arena. Eat the light, grow longer, and coil around rival serpents to cut them off — a trapped rival bursts into harvestable light, but one touch with your head ends the run. Rivals think in three styles — grazers, hunters, and trappers — so every run plays different.
